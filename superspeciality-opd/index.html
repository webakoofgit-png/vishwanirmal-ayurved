Superspeciality OPD – Expert Consultations for Advanced Healthcare

At our Superspeciality OPD, we bring together expert doctors, advanced diagnostic facilities, and personalized treatment plans under one roof.
This department is designed to provide specialized consultation and care for patients with complex or chronic health conditions that need focused medical attention from experienced specialists.

Our aim is to deliver comprehensive, accurate, and high-quality healthcare, ensuring that every patient receives the right guidance, timely diagnosis, and effective treatment — all in a single, well-organized clinical setup.

What is a Superspeciality OPD?

A Superspeciality Out-Patient Department (OPD) is a dedicated wing of a hospital or clinic where patients can consult highly qualified specialists for specific health concerns.
Unlike a general OPD, superspeciality OPDs focus on particular organ systems or diseases and are managed by doctors who have advanced training and expertise in their fields.

This allows for:

  • -Accurate diagnosis of complicated cases

  • -Focused treatment planning using modern and/or Ayurvedic protocols

  • -Early detection and prevention of complications

  • -One-on-one specialist attention without the need for hospitalization

Common Superspeciality OPDs Offered

1.Ayurved General Medicine

Ayurveda General Medicine, also called Kayachikitsa, deals with the prevention and treatment of common health problems using natural methods.
It focuses on balancing the three doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), improving digestion, removing toxins, and strengthening the body from within.

Treatments include:

  • -Herbal medicines and classical Ayurvedic formulations

  • -Detox therapies like Panchakarma

  • -Personalized diet and lifestyle guidance

  • -Yoga and pranayama for better healing

This branch covers a wide range of conditions like digestive issues, fevers, skin diseases, joint pain, metabolic and lifestyle disorders, helping restore overall health and balance.

2.Gynecologist

Gynecology is the branch of medicine that focuses on the health and well-being of the female reproductive system.
A gynecologist provides care for women through every stage of life — from puberty and pregnancy to menopause and beyond.

Common Gynecology Services:

  • -Menstrual and hormonal disorder treatment

  • -Fertility and conception support

  • -Antenatal & postnatal care

  • -PCOD / PCOS and thyroid management

  • -Cervical screening and preventive health checkups

  • -Menopause care and counseling

  • -General reproductive health consultations

Gynecology OPD ensures that women receive specialized, confidential, and compassionate care for all their health needs.

3.Obesity

Obesity is a condition where excess body fat gets accumulated in the body, leading to weight that is higher than what is considered healthy.
It usually develops due to unhealthy eating habits, lack of physical activity, stress, hormonal changes, or genetic factors.

Obesity is more than a cosmetic issue — it increases the risk of many health problems like diabetes, heart disease, joint pain, high blood pressure, and hormonal imbalance.
Common signs include rapid weight gain, tiredness, shortness of breath, and difficulty with daily activities.

Ayurvedic Approach:

In Ayurveda, obesity is called “Sthoulya”. Treatment focuses on:

  • -Balancing metabolism (Agni)

  • -Removing excess toxins (Ama)

  • -Correcting diet and lifestyle

  • -Using herbal medicines, detox therapies (Panchakarma), yoga & exercise

 

4.Infertility

Infertility is when a couple is unable to conceive naturally after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse.
It can affect both men and women due to hormonal imbalances, lifestyle issues, stress, genetic factors, or reproductive system problems.

Common Causes:

  • -Irregular ovulation or blocked fallopian tubes

  • -Low sperm count or motility

  • -Thyroid disorders, PCOD/PCOS, endometriosis

  • -Obesity, stress, and unhealthy habits

Ayurvedic Approach:

Ayurveda focuses on removing toxins, balancing hormones and doshas, and strengthening the reproductive system through Panchakarma, herbal medicines, proper diet, and lifestyle changes.
With the right care, many couples can conceive naturally and safely.

5.Thyroid Disorders

Thyroid disorders occur when the thyroid gland — a small butterfly-shaped gland in the neck — doesn’t produce the right amount of hormones. These hormones control metabolism, growth, and energy levels, so any imbalance affects the entire body.

Common Types:

  • -Hypothyroidism – Underactive thyroid causing fatigue, weight gain, hair fall, and cold intolerance.

  • -Hyperthyroidism – Overactive thyroid causing weight loss, anxiety, rapid heartbeat, and irritability.

Ayurvedic Approach:

Ayurveda aims to balance metabolism and correct hormonal disturbances by:

  • -Detoxifying the body

  • -Using herbal medicines and dietary changes

  • -Regulating lifestyle and stress

  • -Supporting the thyroid through Panchakarma and yoga

 

6.Heart & Diabetes

Heart diseases and Diabetes are closely linked lifestyle disorders that affect millions worldwide.
Diabetes occurs when the body cannot properly use or produce insulin, leading to high blood sugar. Over time, this can damage blood vessels and increase the risk of heart problems like high blood pressure, blockages, and heart attacks.

Common Concerns:

  • -High or fluctuating blood sugar levels

  • -Obesity and lack of physical activity

  • -High cholesterol and blood pressure

  • -Stress, smoking, and unhealthy diet

Treatment includes:

  • -Herbal formulations to regulate sugar and cholesterol

  • -Detox therapies to clear blockages

  • -Personalized diet, yoga, and lifestyle changes

 

7.Spine Care

Spine Care focuses on maintaining the health, strength, and flexibility of the spinal column, which plays a crucial role in posture, movement, and nervous system function.
Spine problems can arise due to poor posture, injuries, lifestyle habits, aging, or underlying medical conditions.

Common Spine Issues:

  • -Back and neck pain

  • -Slip disc or herniated disc

  • -Sciatica and nerve compression

  • -Spondylosis and stiffness

  • -Postural problems

Ayurvedic Approach:

  • -Panchakarma therapies like Kati Basti, Abhyanga, and Basti

  • -Herbal medicines and external applications

  • -Gentle yoga and lifestyle modifications

  • -Strengthening supportive muscles

8.Cancer

Cancer is a disease in which abnormal cells grow and multiply uncontrollably, damaging healthy tissues and organs. It can occur in almost any part of the body and often develops silently over time.

Common Causes & Risk Factors:

  • -Genetic mutations

  • -Unhealthy lifestyle and diet

  • -Tobacco and alcohol use

  • -Environmental toxins

  • -Chronic infections and stress

Ayurvedic Approach:

-Strengthening immunity and supporting healthy cell growth

  • -Detoxifying the body through Panchakarma

  • -Herbal formulations with anti-inflammatory and rejuvenating properties

  • -Dietary and lifestyle corrections

  • -Supportive therapies to improve quality of life

 

9.Respiratory Diseases

Respiratory diseases affect the lungs and airways, making it difficult to breathe properly. These conditions may be acute (short-term) like infections or chronic (long-term) like asthma or COPD. They can occur due to pollution, allergies, infections, smoking, or weakened immunity.

Common Respiratory Conditions

  • -Asthma – Narrowing of airways causing wheezing and breathlessness

  • -Allergic Rhinitis – Sneezing, runny nose, and congestion due to allergens

  • -Bronchitis – Inflammation of bronchial tubes with persistent cough

  • -Sinusitis – Blockage and infection of sinuses

  • -COPD / Emphysema – Long-term lung damage leading to breathing difficulty

10. Skin Diseases – Complete Care for Healthy, Glowing Skin

Skin diseases include a wide range of conditions that affect the skin’s health, appearance, and function.
They can be caused by allergies, infections, lifestyle habits, pollution, stress, or hormonal changes, and may appear as rashes, itching, dryness, pigmentation, or other visible changes.

Common Skin Conditions:

  • -Acne, pimples, and pigmentation

  • -Psoriasis and eczema

  • -Fungal and bacterial infections

  • -Urticaria (hives) and allergic rashes

  • -Hair fall and scalp problems

 

11.Ayurvedic Beauty & Cosmetology

Ayurvedic Beauty & Cosmetology enhances natural beauty through holistic, herbal, and traditional Ayurvedic techniques.
Unlike chemical treatments, Ayurveda works from the inside out, balancing doshas, purifying the body, and nourishing the skin and hair.

Common Treatments:

  • -Herbal facials & skin rejuvenation

  • -Hair & scalp therapies

  • -Anti-acne & pigmentation care

  • -Panchakarma for detox & glow

  • -Bridal & pre-wedding beauty care

  • -Natural anti-aging therapies

Ayurvedic Approach

  • -Uses herbs & oils as per skin type (Vata, Pitta, Kapha)

  • -Focuses on diet, lifestyle & internal cleansing

  • -Promotes healthy, long-lasting beauty & radiant glow naturally

12.Pediatric

Pediatrics is the branch of medicine that focuses on the health, growth, and development of children — from newborns to teenagers.
A pediatrician provides preventive care, diagnosis, and treatment for childhood illnesses, while also guiding parents on nutrition, vaccination, and healthy development.

Common Pediatric Services:

  • -Regular health check-ups & growth monitoring

  • -Immunization & vaccination programs

  • -Treatment for common infections and seasonal illnesses

  • -Guidance on nutrition, sleep, and behavioral issues

  • -Specialized care for allergies, asthma, or chronic conditions

Ayurveda also supports pediatric care through natural immunity boosters like Suvarnaprashan, gentle herbal remedies, and holistic lifestyle guidance for healthy growth.

13.Piles & Fistula

Piles (Hemorrhoids) are swollen blood vessels in the rectal area that can cause pain, itching, bleeding, and discomfort—especially during bowel movements. They are often linked to chronic constipation, straining, or an unhealthy lifestyle.

Fistula is an abnormal tunnel between the anal canal and the skin near the anus, usually caused by infections or abscesses. It may lead to pain, swelling, discharge, and recurrent infections if left untreated.

Ayurvedic Approach:

Ayurveda focuses on treating the root cause, not just the symptoms, through:

  • -Herbal and natural medicines

  • -Kshar Sutra therapy (a minimally invasive Ayurvedic procedure)

  • -Panchakarma and detox therapies

  • -Dietary changes and lifestyle correction to prevent recurrence

14.De-addiction Treatment

De-addiction treatment helps individuals overcome dependence on substances such as alcohol, tobacco, or drugs and regain control of their physical and mental health.
Addiction affects both the body and mind, often leading to serious health, emotional, and social problems if left untreated.

Common Addictions Treated:

  • -Alcohol and smoking

  • -Gutkha, tobacco, and nicotine products

  • -Drug and substance abuse

  • -Screen or behavioral addictions (in some cases)

Ayurvedic Approach:

Ayurveda focuses on detoxifying the body, calming the mind, and restoring inner balance through:

  • -Panchakarma and herbal detox therapies

  • -Rasayana (rejuvenation) treatments to repair damage

  • -Herbal formulations to reduce cravings and withdrawal symptoms

  • -Yoga, meditation, and counseling for mental strength

  • -Lifestyle guidance for long-term recovery

15.Eye Care

Eye Care focuses on maintaining clear vision and healthy eyes through regular check-ups, early diagnosis, and proper treatment of eye conditions.
Modern lifestyle factors like screen time, pollution, stress, and aging can affect eye health, making routine care essential.

Common Eye Problems:

  • -Refractive errors (near/farsightedness, astigmatism)

  • -Eye strain and headaches

  • -Dry eyes and allergies

  • -Cataract and glaucoma

  • -Infections and redness

Ayurvedic Approach:

Ayurveda emphasizes nourishing and cleansing the eyes through:

  • -Netra Tarpana and other Ayurvedic eye therapies

  • -Herbal formulations and medicated ghee

  • -Lifestyle and dietary guidance to protect vision

  • -Eye exercises and relaxation practices

16.Arthritis

Arthritis is a condition that causes inflammation and pain in the joints, making movement difficult and uncomfortable. It can affect people of all ages and commonly involves the knees, hands, hips, and spine.
The most common types are Osteoarthritis (wear and tear of joints) and Rheumatoid Arthritis (autoimmune inflammation).

Common Symptoms:

  • -Joint pain, swelling, and stiffness

  • -Reduced flexibility and difficulty in movement

  • -Morning stiffness or pain after rest

  • -Warmth or redness around joints in some cases

Ayurvedic Approach:

In Ayurveda, arthritis is often linked to imbalances in Vata dosha and toxin accumulation. Treatment focuses on:

  • -Reducing inflammation and pain through herbal medicines

  • -Panchakarma therapies like Abhyanga, Basti, and Swedana

  • -Dietary and lifestyle changes to support joint health

  • -Gentle yoga and exercises to improve mobility

17.Garbha Sanskar

Garbha Sanskar is the ancient Ayurvedic practice of nurturing the baby in the womb through a positive physical, mental, and spiritual environment.
It focuses on the holistic well-being of both mother and baby during pregnancy, ensuring healthy growth and balanced development.

Key Aspects:

  • -Balanced diet and lifestyle for a healthy pregnancy

  • -Yoga, meditation, and music to keep the mind calm and joyful

  • -Positive thoughts, reading, and bonding with the baby

  • -Ayurvedic herbs and supplements under expert care

  • -Traditional rituals to create a nurturing environment

Ayurvedic View:

Ayurveda believes a baby’s physical, mental, and emotional traits are shaped by the mother’s nutrition, state of mind, and surroundings during pregnancy.
Garbha Sanskar helps lay a strong foundation for the baby’s health, intelligence, and emotional well-being even before birth.

18.Ayurvedic Dietician

An Ayurvedic Dietician helps design personalized food and lifestyle plans based on your body type (Prakriti), current imbalances (Doshas), and health goals.
Unlike modern diet plans that focus only on calories, Ayurveda emphasizes seasonal foods, digestive strength (Agni), and overall balance to maintain good health naturally.

Key Focus Areas:

  • -Identifying your body constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha)

  • -Recommending suitable foods, herbs, and meal timings

  • -Supporting weight management, digestion, and immunity

  • -Offering guidance for specific health conditions through diet

  • -Encouraging mindful eating and seasonal adjustments

Ayurvedic diet planning aims to nourish the body, balance the mind, and prevent disease — creating long-lasting wellness through natural, wholesome food.
